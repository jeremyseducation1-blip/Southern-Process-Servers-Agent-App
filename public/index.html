Route

Load your open cases, tap them in the order you'll drive, then open the whole route in Apple Maps. At each stop tap "Log attempt (I'm here)" — it records the attempt with the time and your location whether or not the car is turned off.

Miles for this route

Calculates home → your stops in order → back home, over the road. It's an estimate; if your odometer says different, type that number before saving.

Return

Scan the served/returned paper in Genius Scan, export it as a PDF, then upload that PDF here. It attaches to this case right away — no email gets sent to Kevin from this step. Exception: for an alias case with Scott Weiss as attorney, this return still gets bundled and sent automatically when you send the affidavit below.

We track papers, not just cases — this case has more than one defendant. Check off which one(s) this specific return covers. Only those get marked returned; the others stay open.

Affidavit

Log attempts from Search Cases (below) against the case they're for. Once a case has all 3 attempts (date + note each, no future dates), come back here with that same case number + defendant typed in the Case section above, and the status dropdown unlocks automatically.
